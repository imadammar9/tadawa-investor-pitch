The problem

The inefficiency of healthcare

Modern healthcare was not designed around patients; it was fundamentally built around hospitals, doctors, and the insurance system. Today, millions of patients enter the healthcare system through the wrong door. They bypass primary care, choosing to visit specialists directly when they should initially see a family physician or a primary healthcare provider.

Higher costsfor payers
Fragmented carefor patients
Poorer outcomesfor everyone
